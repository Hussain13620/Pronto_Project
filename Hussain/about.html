OUR SOLUTION

One platform for your everyday household needs.

KaamKaaj brings customers and household service providers together through a simple digital platform.

We simplify the process of discovering services, scheduling work, and ensuring a seamless experience for both home owners and service providers.

WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT

Instant help, not a waiting list.

Most services ask you to pick a time slot and wait. KaamKaaj works the other way around — the moment you raise a request, every available worker nearby is notified immediately.

1

You Raise a Request

Tell us what you need — sweeping, mopping, laundry, and more.

2

Nearby Workers Get Notified

Every available worker in your area instantly sees the request.

3

First Free Worker Accepts

Whoever is free accepts and heads to you right away — no scheduling, no waiting.
